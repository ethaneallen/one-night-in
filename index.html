How to Play

What this game is

You play a paranormal investigator hired by an unnamed executor to spend one night locked inside Ashgrove House, a Gothic mansion abandoned since 1974. The executor will pay $25,000 for your verdict at sunrise. Your job is to determine, using real-world ghost-hunting tools, whether the house is actually haunted.

Every new game randomly picks one of three truths. You are not told which. You have until dawn (5:30 AM) to figure it out.

The three possible answers
HAUNTED

The full legend is real. Twelve entities live in the house, each detectable by specific instruments. The tools don't lie.

PARTIALLY HAUNTED

One real ghost. Everything else is coincidence, wiring, and suggestion. Separating signal from noise is the puzzle.

DEBUNKED

Nothing. All "phenomena" are drafts, pipes, old wiring, and your own pareidolia. Prove it with thermal readings and environmental explanations.

The four things you do
  1. Listen to Calder. The groundskeeper tells you the family's history when you arrive. Some of what he says is wrong — he's repeating what he was told. Documents in the library, study, and kitchen contradict him. Catching him in his inconsistencies pays a bonus at the verdict.
  2. Use your instruments. After Calder leaves at sundown, nine tools appear at the bottom of the screen. Hover any one to see what it does. Click to use. Visit every room. Evidence logs automatically to the journal (J).
  3. Sit still. Speak aloud. Every room has Rest, Sit in Silence, and Call Out actions. Use them. Not every kind of evidence comes through an instrument — sometimes the house only answers when you stop looking at it.
  4. Deliver your verdict. At sunrise, click the desk in the entry hall. Pick Haunted, Partially Haunted, or Debunked. You will not be told if you were right until after you commit.
The nine instruments
K-II EMF MeterFive LEDs. Lights up near ghosts — also old wiring. Cross-check with thermal.
Spirit BoxScans radio frequencies. Words pop out of the static. Log the ones you think are meaningful — others are noise.
OvilusPassive. Speaks a word on its own when an entity is near. Creepier than the spirit box because you didn't ask.
SLS CameraDraws a skeletal stick-figure over anything it reads as human-shaped. Some furniture false-positives.
EVP RecorderPlace in a room, leave for 5 in-game minutes, review the waveform. The only tool that catches certain entities.
Thermal CameraCold spots = ghost activity. Hot sources = mundane (pipes, wiring). Log debunks — on a debunked run they pay the most.
EM PumpPlace in a room. Baits entities there and unlocks their detection on tools that would normally miss them. Raises aggression.
REM PodProximity alarm. Place and leave. Sounds from any other room when something crosses its field.
Film CameraTake photographs of any room. Review them in the Gallery — sometimes an anomaly appears that was not there when the shutter clicked. Classify each as real or mundane.
Danger: the aggression meter

Every time you provoke the house — knocking on walls, using the EM pump, getting a threat from the spirit box — a hidden meter rises. The current tier is shown at the top of the screen:

QUIET WATCHING CLOSE IN THE ROOM

At Watching, small atmospheric scares fire. At Close, you may be forced into a "dodge-or-die" prompt where the wrong choice kills you. At In the Room on a haunted run, your next action can end the investigation permanently. Rest lowers the meter. Sit in Silence raises it slightly but may reward you with unique evidence.

The story you're uncovering

Ashgrove House was built in 1887 by Elias Ashgrove over the foundation of the 1851 Meeting-House, which burned with nineteen souls inside. His wife Margaret held séances. His daughter-in-law Evelyn died in the nursery in 1923. The twins Henry and Clara disappeared in 1935. Adeline, the last occupant, recorded tapes in the study until 1974.

At exactly 12:00 AM in every game, something slides under the front door. Read it. It tells you what the whole night is actually about.

How to earn the most money
  • Correct verdict → the base $25,000 fee
  • Catch Calder in his lies → +$500 per contradiction, up to 5
  • Use 4+ tools → +$1,500 coverage bonus; 6+ → +$3,000
  • Name the right entity on the verdict screen → up to +$9,000 (if you found the supporting documents)
  • Evidence from each tool category scales independently — diversify
