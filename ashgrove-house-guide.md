# 👻 How to Play — One Night In… Ashgrove House

*This is the player's guide for **Chapter I** of the **One Night In…** anthology.*

## 🏚️ What This Game Is

You play a **paranormal investigator** hired to spend one night locked inside **Ashgrove House** — a Gothic mansion abandoned since 1974.

**Your mission:** Determine if the house is actually haunted using real ghost-hunting tools.

**Your reward:** $25,000 if you're right.

**Your deadline:** Dawn arrives at 5:30 AM.

Every new game randomly picks one of three truths. You won't know which one. Good luck! 😊

## 🔮 The Three Possible Answers

### 👻 Haunted
The full legend is real. Twelve entities live in the house, each detectible by specific instruments. The tools don't lie.

### 🤔 Partially Haunted
One real ghost. Everything else is coincidence, wiring, and suggestion. Separating signal from noise is the puzzle.

### 🚫 Debunked
Nothing supernatural. All "phenomena" are drafts, pipes, old wiring, and your own pareidolia. Prove it with thermal readings and environmental explanations.

## 🎮 What You'll Do

#### 1️⃣ Listen to Calder
The groundskeeper tells you the family history when you arrive. Some of what he says is wrong — he's repeating what he heard.

👉 **Action:** Document everything in your history and fact-check his inconsistencies. You'll earn a bonus for catching his lies!

#### 2️⃣ Use Your Instruments
After Calder leaves at sundown, nine tools appear in the basement. Move them around the house and use them to gather evidence.

👉 **Action:** Click to use each tool. Write down your observations in your journal (press J).

#### 3️⃣ Rest & Communicate
Don't just rely on machines! Take breaks with *Rest*, sit in *Silence*, and *Call Out* to the house.

👉 **Action:** Not every kind of evidence comes through an instrument — sometimes the house only answers when you talk to it.

#### 4️⃣ Deliver Your Verdict
At sunrise, go to the desk in the entry hall. Pick **Haunted**, **Partially Haunted**, or **Debunked**.

👉 **Action:** Make your choice and find out if you were right!

## 🔧 The Nine Instruments

### 📊 K-II EMF Meter
Detects electromagnetic fields. Five LEDs light up near ghosts — but also near odd wiring. Cross-check with thermal readings to be sure.

### 🎙️ Ovilus
Passive device that speaks a word on its own when an entity is near. Creepier than the spirit box because you didn't ask.

### 🎧 EVP Recorder
Place in a room, leave for 3 game-minutes, then review the waveform. The only tool that catches certain entities.

### ⚡ EM Pump
Place in a room to emit and unlock ghost discretion on tools that would normally miss them. Warning: Raises aggression!

### 📻 Spirit Box
Scans radio frequencies. Words pop out of the static. Log the ones you think are meaningful — ignore the noise.

### 🎥 SLS Camera
Renders skeletal stick-figures over anything it reads as human-shaped. Watch out for false-positives on furniture!

### 🌡️ Thermal Camera
Cold spots = ghost activity. Hot sources = minimize (pipes, wiring). Log datapoints for better payouts.

### 🚨 REM Pod
Proximity alarm. Place and leave it. Sounds from any other room when something crosses its field.

### 📷 Film Camera
35mm sepia film. Take photographs of any room; review in the Gallery to develop them. Anomalies (figures, orbs, smudges, mist) may appear in the frame — classify each as real or mundane. Cross-reference with your other evidence.

## ⚠️ Danger: The Aggression Meter

The more you provoke the house, the more aggressive it becomes.

**Actions that raise aggression:**
- Knocking on walls 🔊
- Using the EM pump ⚡
- Getting threats from the spirit box 📻

**Aggression Levels:**
- 👀 **Watching** → Small atmospheric scares
- 🤏 **Close** → You may be forced into a dodge-or-die prompt (choose wrong = game over!)
- 😰 **In the Room** → On a haunted run, your next action can end everything

**How to calm down:**
- 😌 *Rest* lowers the meter
- 🤐 *Sit in Silence* raises it slightly but may reward unique evidence

## 📖 The Story You're Uncovering

**1887** — Elias Ashgrove builds the house over the foundation of an 1850 Meeting House that burned with 19 souls inside.

**1920s** — His wife Margaret holds séances. His daughter-in-law Evelyn dies in the nursery in 1923.

**1935** — The twins Henry and Clara disappear.

**Later** — Adeline, the last occupant, evicts pets from the walls and goes mad.

**Every night at midnight** — Something slides under the front door. Read it. It tells you what the whole night is actually about. 💌
