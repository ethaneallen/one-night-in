# One Night In… Ashgrove House — Walkthrough & Cheatsheet

*Chapter I of the One Night In… anthology.*

**Spoilers ahead.** This is the full solution for all three truths.

---

## 1. How to win at the highest payout

Every game randomly picks one of three truths: **Haunted**, **Partially Haunted**, or **Debunked**. You don't know which. Your job is to deliver the correct verdict at sunrise.

Max payout (~$54,500) requires:
- Correct verdict
- All 5 Calder contradictions caught (+$2,500)
- 6+ different tools used (+$3,000)
- Correct entity named on the verdict screen (+$4,000–$9,000)
- Heavy evidence in the categories the truth rewards

Minimum to survive: stay below max aggression on Haunted runs, read the midnight letter, don't skip Calder.

## 2. Opening sequence (7:00 PM – Calder leaves, ~7:45 PM)

1. **Let Calder finish his tour.** Skipping him forfeits up to $2,500 in bonuses. He makes 5 specific claims — all are contradicted by documents in the house.
2. **Calder's 5 claims to catch:**
   - Evelyn "fell" (nursery) → contradicted by the **coroner's report**
   - Twins "drowned" in the pond → contradicted by the **1940 Gazette clipping**

   - Executor "hasn't signed anything since 1902" → contradicted by **midnight letter**
   - Cellar door is "just stuck" → contradicted by **midnight letter**
3. When Calder leaves, the front door bolts. Your 9 instruments appear.

## 8. Verdict-by-verdict strategy

### Haunted (ghosts are real)
- Visit every room at least once — every entity is worth a capture.
- Place EVP recorders early (each needs 5+ min to develop).
- Use the EM Pump in the wine cellar, master bedroom, and nursery — it unlocks entities on tools that wouldn't normally see them.
- At 4 AM, pick a pact choice: **"Yes"** caps aggression at 3 for the rest of the night (safest). "Name Eliza" also counts as full identification.
- Naming bonus: **Eliza** + tape + fire clipping + séance carving = **+$9,000**.

### Partially Haunted (one real ghost, rest is noise)
- EVP is king (+$2,500 per whisper, up to $5,000).
- Log thermal debunks aggressively (pipes, flues).
- The Quiet Twin (nursery) and Tape Voice (study) are your two real signals.
- Naming bonus: **"Quiet Twin" / "Henry" / "Clara"** after seeing the entity = **+$7,000**.

### Debunked (nothing is real)
- Thermal debunks pay the most: $1,000 each, cap $5,000.
- Environmental explanations pay $500 each (describe why the noise is mundane).
- The tape still plays, but it's just Adeline.
- Naming bonus: type **"None"** or **"Nothing"** or **"Nobody"** on the verdict screen = **+$4,000**.

## 9. Staying alive

**Aggression tiers** (watch the HUD label):
- QUIET (0–3) — baseline
- WATCHING (4–6) — atmospheric scares
- CLOSE (7–8) — "dodge-or-die" threat prompts (pick the **calm** option, not the defiant one)
- IN THE ROOM (9–10) — on Haunted runs, next provocation kills you

**Things that raise aggression:**
- EM Pump, threats on Spirit Box, knocking, Call Out, Sit in Silence, shouting "come at me bro"

**Things that lower it:**
- **Rest** (45-in-game-min cooldown, drops 2 points) — use Rest button in any room
- Time without provocation

**At CLOSE or IN THE ROOM, if a threat prompt fires:**
- Always pick the calm / compliant option. "Leave the room" beats "confront it."
- On Haunted, the wrong choice on IN THE ROOM is instant death.

## 10. The film camera

Taking a photo rolls for one of four anomaly types:
- **Figure** — real ghost silhouette (Haunted/Partial only, when entity is in room)
- **Orb** — 1–3 floating lights (mostly dust — but sometimes real)
- **Smudge** — dark smear (fingerprint on lens — or ectoplasm)
- **Mist** — pale haze (breath in cold air — or a presence)

In the Gallery, each anomaly photo prompts **"Log as evidence"** or **"Dismiss as mundane."** Wrong calls still go in the journal — correct calls score toward the verdict.

**How to judge:** the review screen now shows every other piece of evidence collected in that same room. Multiple corroborating readings (EMF, EVP, cold spots) = probably real. Empty room = probably dust.

## 12. Optimal play order (Haunted, no deaths)

1. Greet Calder, let him finish the full walkthrough
2. When he leaves, **immediately read the coroner's report + pond clipping + sealed letter** (catches 3/5 contradictions)
3. Place EVP recorders in nursery, library, master bedroom
4. Use K-II + Ovilus + SLS + Thermal in every room, top to bottom
5. Return to EVP placements after 5 min — review waveforms
6. Read remaining docs in study + parlor; note Eliza's name
7. At midnight: stay near entry hall to witness the letter slide under
8. Between 12:00–3:00 AM: visit each scripted scare room
9. Use EM Pump in wine cellar (unlocks The Hands on everything)
10. 4:00 AM confrontation — choose **"Yes"** OR **"Name Eliza"** (both good)
11. Rest 1–2 times to keep aggression below 7
12. 5:30 AM sunrise — go to desk, verdict **Haunted**, name **Eliza**
